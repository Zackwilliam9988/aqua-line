Complete Range of Dosing Pumps

Etatron offers a full spectrum of dosing pump technologies — from compact solenoid diaphragm pumps for cooling towers and boiler treatment to motor-driven units for high-volume industrial chemical injection.

AquaLine specifies pump models, materials of construction, and accessory packages matched to your chemical compatibility, flow rate, and pressure requirements.

  • Solenoid pumps — PKX, DLX, eONE, and BT series
  • Motor-driven pumps for high-capacity applications
  • Peristaltic pumps for shear-sensitive chemicals
  • Spare parts and service support nationwide

Control Instruments

Controllers integrate with Etatron dosing pumps for closed-loop control in cooling water, wastewater, and process applications across Pakistan's industrial sector.

  • Sensor accessories and calibration support

Proportional Dosing

Control & Regulation Instruments

Advanced regulation systems deliver proportional dosing based on real-time process parameters — including flow-paced injection, multi-parameter control, and remote setpoint adjustment.

Regulation equipment is essential where chemical demand varies with production rate, feed water quality, or seasonal operating conditions.

  • Proportional dosing from process signals
  • Flow-paced and multi-parameter control
  • Integration with plant instrumentation
  • Engineering support for control logic setup
