Deep Well

4″ Submersible Electric Pumps

Stairs 4-inch ST series pumps use high-quality materials and a floating impeller design that reduces starting torque and tolerates sand in the water — delivering years of dependable borehole and well service.

The SP stainless steel range offers corrosion and abrasion resistance for aggressive water sources, developed with efficient hydraulic impeller and diffuser design for high performance.

  • 4″ ST submersible electric pumps
  • 4″ SP stainless steel submersible range
  • Floating impeller for sand tolerance
  • Suitable for water supply and irrigation
In-Line Design

SB, SBI & SBN Vertical Multistage Pumps

SB, SBI, and SBN vertical multistage centrifugal pumps are powered by standard electric motors with the pump shaft directly coupled. The in-line suction and discharge ports provide a compact footprint and straightforward installation.

Multiple stages and capacities are available for reverse osmosis feed, filtration, pressure boosting, HVAC, cooling, and general industrial water treatment duties.

  • In-line suction and discharge ports
  • Head up to 325 m, capacity to 90 m³/h (50 Hz)
  • Detachable cartridge mechanical seal
  • RO feed, filtration, and pressure boosting
Compact Install

CB & CBI Horizontal Multistage Pumps

CB and CBI horizontal multistage centrifugal pumps mount impellers directly on the motor shaft with the pump housing attached to a base plate — ideal where vertical space is limited.

A high-quality mechanical seal is included as standard. The design suits small plant rooms, skid packages, and auxiliary water systems in industrial facilities.

  • Horizontal multistage on motor shaft
  • Compact base-plate mounting
  • Integrated mechanical shaft seal
  • Ideal for confined installation spaces
