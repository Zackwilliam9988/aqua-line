Pulping

Pulp Mill Process Chemistry

Pulp mill operations demand consistent chemical performance through bleaching, washing, and stock preparation stages. Amazon Papyrus programs are designed to support brightness targets, pulp quality, and washing efficiency while managing chemical consumption.

PLUMPK technical teams work with mill personnel to optimize application points, monitor performance, and adjust programs as furnish and operating conditions change.

  • Bleaching and washing stage chemicals
  • Stock preparation support
  • Brightness and pulp quality optimization
  • On-site troubleshooting and trials
Paper Machine

Wet-End & Retention Programs

Paper machine wet-end chemistry directly affects retention, drainage, formation, and machine speed. Amazon Papyrus retention and drainage aids help mills reduce fiber loss, improve sheet uniformity, and maintain stable operation across grade changes.

Programs are tailored to furnish type — recycled, virgin, or mixed — and integrated with other wet-end additives for compatible, reliable performance.

  • Retention and drainage polymers
  • Wet-end additive integration
  • Improved formation and runnability
  • Grade-change program support
System Cleanliness

Defoamers & Deposit Control

Foam, pitch, and organic deposits reduce machine efficiency and product quality. Amazon Papyrus defoamers and deposit control agents protect wire, press, and dryer sections while keeping white water and stock systems clean.

PLUMPK provides application audits to identify deposit sources and recommend targeted chemical and operational interventions.

  • Silicone and non-silicone defoamers
  • Pitch and stickies control
  • Wire and felt deposit management
  • System cleanliness audits
Water Circuits

Mill Water System Treatment

Closed water loops in modern paper mills require careful chemical management to control microbiological growth, corrosion, scaling, and foam. Integrated water system treatment protects equipment and maintains consistent process water quality.

PLUMPK links mill water programs with broader water treatment expertise — from intake clarification through effluent treatment — for a complete site-wide approach.

  • White water and circuit treatment
  • Microbiological and foam control
  • Corrosion and scaling inhibition
  • Integration with mill ETP programs
