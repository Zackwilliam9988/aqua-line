24/7 Emergency & Complete Plumbing Solutions

Pakistan's Trusted Plumbing Experts & 24/7 Emergency Service

PLUMPK (Plumbers Pakistan) is the country's premier professional plumbing service provider, delivering prompt, reliable, and clean residential, commercial, and emergency plumbing solutions.

🚨 24/7 Emergency Service in Rawalpindi & Islamabad: Burst pipes, overflowing overhead tanks, or sudden geyser leakages require immediate intervention. Our mobile emergency response vans are always available 24/7 across Rawalpindi and Islamabad with an average 30 to 45 minute arrival time. Each mobile unit is equipped with acoustic leak detectors, motorized drain snakes, and original PPRC pipes and brass fittings.

"Prompt Response. Skilled Craftsmanship. Honest Pricing." From modern Pakistani washroom renovations and kitchen sink drainage to rooftop overhead water tank piping and automatic booster pumps, our certified plumbers take pride in clean, long-lasting workmanship.
