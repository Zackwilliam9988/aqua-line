Ultrapure Water

Electrodeionization (EDI)

Continuous electrodeionization modules produce ultrapure water without the chemical regeneration required by conventional mixed-bed systems — reducing operator workload, chemical handling, and downtime.

Canpure EDI modules are available in a range of flow capacities with standard electrical specifications compatible with Pakistani industrial power systems. PLUMPK provides sizing, integration, and commissioning support for pharmaceutical, power, and electronics applications.

  • No salt or chemical regeneration required
  • Counter-current flow design for maximum efficiency
  • Low energy consumption compared to mixed-bed systems
  • Compact footprint for tight utility rooms
  • Consistent water quality with minimal operator attention
Pre-Treatment

Ultrafiltration (UF) Systems

Ultrafiltration provides reliable removal of turbidity, colloids, and microbiological matter — protecting reverse osmosis and EDI stages from fouling and extending membrane life.

Canpure UF systems are applied as RO pre-treatment, process water filtration, and polishing stages in multi-barrier ultrapure water trains across pharmaceutical, food, and industrial plants.

  • RO and EDI pre-treatment protection
  • Turbidity and colloid removal
  • Stable feed water for downstream membranes
  • Skid-mounted packages for integration
Fine Filtration

Microfiltration (MF) Systems

Microfiltration removes fine suspended solids and provides consistent particulate control upstream of high-pressure membrane stages and electrodeionization modules.

MF is deployed where feed water variability demands robust solids removal — supporting reliable operation across seasonal and source water changes common in Pakistani industrial applications.

  • Fine particulate and solids removal
  • Upstream protection for RO and EDI
  • Consistent filtrate quality
  • Engineering support for system integration
Wastewater Reuse

Membrane Bioreactor (MBR)

Membrane bioreactor technology combines biological treatment with membrane separation — producing high-quality effluent suitable for reuse or compliant discharge in a compact footprint.

Canpure MBR solutions support industrial wastewater reuse, retrofit of conventional biological stages, and sites where land availability and effluent quality targets demand advanced membrane separation.

  • Biological treatment with membrane separation
  • High-quality effluent for reuse applications
  • Compact footprint vs conventional clarifiers
  • Suitable for industrial wastewater streams
