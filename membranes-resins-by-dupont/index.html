Reverse Osmosis

FilmTec™ RO Membrane Elements

FilmTec™ reverse osmosis elements deliver high rejection and high productivity for brackish water and seawater desalination — available in standard 4-inch and 8-inch configurations with proven field performance across power, municipal, food, and industrial plants.

AquaLine helps customers select the right element model for feed water quality, recovery targets, and operating pressure — and supplies membranes for new plants and routine replacement programs.

  • Brackish and seawater RO elements
  • Standard 4-inch and 8-inch configurations
  • High rejection and productivity
  • Proven performance in demanding applications
Selective Separation

Nanofiltration Membranes

DuPont nanofiltration membranes provide selective separation for applications where full RO rejection is not required — including partial softening, organics removal, and specialty process separations.

NF technology complements FilmTec RO systems in multi-stage trains and is applied where operating costs and permeate quality targets favor selective membrane performance.

  • Selective hardness and organics removal
  • Lower operating pressure than full RO
  • Specialty separation applications
  • Integration with RO pre-treatment trains
Ion Exchange

Amberlite™ Ion Exchange Resins

DuPont Amberlite™ resins cover cation, anion, and specialty ion exchange applications — including water softening, demineralization, dealkalization, and process separations in power, pharmaceutical, and industrial water systems.

AquaLine specifies resin types and quantities for softener and DM plant designs — matching conductivity, silica, and hardness targets to the correct resin chemistry.

  • Cation and anion exchange resins
  • Softening and demineralization
  • Dealkalization and specialty separations
  • Mixed-bed and two-bed applications
High Efficiency

Amberjet™ Uniform Particle Resins

Amberjet™ uniform particle resins offer improved hydraulic characteristics and regeneration efficiency in demineralization and polishing applications — supporting consistent effluent quality in high-purity water systems.

Combined with AquaLine's engineering capability for softening and DM plants, DuPont resins provide a complete ion exchange solution from specification through commissioning.

  • Uniform particle resin technology
  • High-efficiency demineralization
  • Polishing and high-purity water systems
  • Supported by AquaLine plant engineering
