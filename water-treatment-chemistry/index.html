Cooling Systems

Cooling Water Treatment

Veolia cooling water treatment products include pre-commission cleaning, flushing, and passivating agents to prepare systems for reliable operation. Inhibitors control scale formation, corrosion, and fouling across open recirculating and closed-loop circuits.

We supply oxidizing and non-oxidizing biocides, biodispersants, and microbiological control systems to protect warm wet surfaces from biofilms and microbiologically influenced corrosion (MIC).

Computer simulation programs optimize treatment based on saturation indices, retention times, and cycles of concentration — balancing technical performance with cost-effectiveness.

  • Pre-commission cleaning & passivation
  • Scale, corrosion & fouling inhibitors
  • Oxidizing & non-oxidizing biocides
  • Simulation-based program optimization
Steam Systems

Boiler Water Treatment

In partnership with Veolia Water Technologies, we offer comprehensive boiler water treatment chemicals for every type of system — from low-pressure steam generators to high-pressure water tube boilers.

  • Volatile & non-volatile oxygen scavengers
  • Phosphate residual & all-polymer programs
  • Alkalinity builders, defoamers & sludge conditioners
  • Filming, neutralizing & combined amines
Membrane Systems

Reverse Osmosis Plant Chemicals

Our Veolia Hydrex™ RO chemicals are compatible with all membrane types, approved by leading manufacturers, and certified to NSF Standard 60.

Highly effective at low dose rates across brackish, seawater, and freshwater feed — performing across the full pH spectrum with polyelectrolyte compatibility.

We also supply fully biodegradable green chemistries with low biofouling potential to maximize membrane performance and extend system life.

  • NSF 60 certified formulations
  • Antiscalants, cleaners & biocides
  • Low-dose, broad pH compatibility
  • Biodegradable green chemistry options
