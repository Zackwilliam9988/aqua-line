Cooling Systems

Cooling Water Treatment

Veolia cooling water conditioning programs incorporate advanced pre-commission cleaning, surface passivation, and heavy-duty polymeric scale and corrosion inhibitors. Formulations protect both open evaporative cooling towers and closed chilled water loops from heat exchanger deposition.

We deploy broad-spectrum oxidizing and non-oxidizing biocide regimens, bio-dispersants, and targeted algaecides to prevent biofilm formation, Legionella proliferation, and microbiologically influenced corrosion (MIC).

Advanced thermodynamic simulation tools calculate saturation indices and optimize cycles of concentration (COC) — balancing operating efficiency with water conservation.

  • Pre-commission cleaning & passivation
  • Scale, corrosion & fouling inhibitors
  • Oxidizing & non-oxidizing biocides
  • Simulation-based program optimization
Steam Systems

Boiler Water Treatment

In alliance with Veolia Water Technologies, we engineer comprehensive boiler internal chemistry programs spanning low-pressure firetube boilers to high-pressure steam turbines and co-generation facilities.

Our solutions prevent internal tube scaling, eliminate dissolved oxygen pitting, and provide full condensate return line neutralization to preserve expensive steam piping assets.

  • Catalyzed DEHA, sulfite & volatile oxygen scavengers
  • Coordinated phosphate & advanced all-polymer dispersants
  • Alkalinity builders, defoamers & sludge conditioners
  • Filming, neutralizing & multi-component neutralizing amines
Membrane Systems

Reverse Osmosis Plant Chemicals

Our Veolia Hydrex™ reverse osmosis chemicals are fully approved by top membrane manufacturers and certified to NSF/ANSI Standard 60 for drinking water and high-purity industrial use.

Engineered for low-dosage injection across high-silica brackish water, seawater intake, and municipal effluents — maintaining stable permeate flux and preventing mineral scaling across variable pH conditions.

Our product portfolio includes environmentally responsible, readily biodegradable antiscalants and CIP cleaners formulated to restore flux without damaging composite membranes.

  • NSF/ANSI Standard 60 certified formulations
  • High-recovery antiscalants, specialty CIP cleaners & biocides
  • Broad spectrum calcium, sulfate & silica fouling prevention
  • Readily biodegradable green chemistry options
