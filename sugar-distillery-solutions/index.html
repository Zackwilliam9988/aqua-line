Sugar Mill

Juice Clarification & Process Aids

Efficient juice clarification is the foundation of sugar recovery and pan house performance. PLUMPK supplies flocculants and process aids tailored to local cane quality, mud load, and clarifier design — helping mills improve clarity, reduce losses, and stabilize downstream operations.

Our technical teams support jar testing, dose optimization, and seasonal start-up to ensure programs perform from the first day of crushing through campaign close.

  • Clarification and flocculant aids for juice treatment
  • pH control and alkalinity management chemicals
  • Mud handling and syrup stage support
  • On-site trials and dose-rate optimization
Boiling House

Evaporator, Pan & Refinery Chemistry

Scaling, foaming, and deposit buildup in evaporators and vacuum pans reduce heat transfer and increase energy consumption. Antiscalant and defoamer programs protect critical surfaces while supporting consistent brix targets and boil-out schedules.

PLUMPK also supports mill sanitation and CIP cleaning programs to maintain hygienic process conditions across the boiling house and refinery stages.

  • Antiscalants for evaporator and pan operations
  • Defoamers for sugar boiling and processing
  • Mill sanitation and CIP cleaning programs
  • Energy-efficient scale and deposit management
Distillery

Molasses Fermentation Solutions

Molasses-based ethanol production depends on healthy yeast, stable fermentation, and controlled foam. PLUMPK supplies fermentation nutrients, antifoam agents, pH buffers, and supplement programs designed for distillery operating conditions in Pakistan.

Programs are adjusted for molasses variability, fermentation vessel design, and production targets — with technical support for troubleshooting stuck fermentations and yield optimization.

  • Fermentation nutrients and yeast health products
  • Antifoam agents for fermentation vessels
  • pH buffers and nutrient supplements
  • Yield optimization and process troubleshooting
Environmental

Spent Wash, Utilities & Effluent Treatment

Distillery operations generate demanding effluent loads and rely on dependable utility water systems. PLUMPK supplies effluent treatment chemicals for spent wash clarification and sludge handling, alongside boiler and cooling water programs for steam and utility circuits.

Where full plant solutions are required, our engineering team designs and commissions ETP systems — integrated with the chemical programs that keep them running reliably.

  • Effluent treatment chemicals for spent wash
  • Cooling water treatment for distillery utilities
  • Boiler water treatment for steam generation
  • Linked ETP design and commissioning support
