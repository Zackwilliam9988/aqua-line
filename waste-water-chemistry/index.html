Effluent Treatment

Clarification & Sedimentation

BESFLOC™ polyelectrolytes improve solids separation in primary and secondary clarification stages — increasing overflow clarity, stabilizing under varying load conditions, and helping plants meet discharge standards consistently.

PLUMPK supports jar testing, polymer selection, and dosing optimization so the right molecular weight and charge density are matched to your effluent characteristics.

  • Industrial wastewater clarification
  • Primary & secondary sedimentation
  • Improved overflow clarity under variable loads
  • On-site jar testing & polymer selection
Solids Handling

Sludge Thickening & Dewatering

Efficient sludge management reduces hauling costs and improves overall ETP economics. BESFLOC™ polymers are formulated for gravity thickeners, belt presses, centrifuges, and vacuum filtration equipment.

Our technical teams work with plant operators to fine-tune dosage, mixing energy, and maturation time for maximum cake dryness and filtrate quality.

  • Gravity thickening applications
  • Belt press & centrifuge dewatering
  • Vacuum filter processes
  • Dosing & mixing optimization support
Raw Water

Canal & Surface Water Treatment

Canal and surface water sources often carry high turbidity and colloidal solids that challenge conventional treatment. BESFLOC™ flocculants accelerate floc formation and improve settling performance in intake and pre-treatment systems.

Programs are tailored for seasonal turbidity swings and varying raw water quality across industrial and municipal applications.

  • Canal & intake water clarification
  • Turbidity & colloid removal
  • Seasonal load flexibility
  • Compatible with coagulant programs
Textile Effluent

Dyeing Industry Decolorization

Textile and dyeing effluents present unique challenges including color, suspended solids, and complex organics. KOLON decolorizer and flocculant combinations are designed for activated sludge and physico-chemical treatment trains.

PLUMPK provides application guidance for textile clusters in Faisalabad, Karachi, Lahore, and other industrial hubs where color removal and sludge dewatering are critical.

  • Dyehouse effluent decolorization
  • Activated sludge process support
  • Combined color & solids removal
  • Textile industry application expertise
