Effluent Treatment

Clarification & Sedimentation

BESFLOC™ polyacrylamide flocculants significantly accelerate solid-liquid separation in primary dissolved air flotation (DAF) units and secondary biological clarifiers. They form dense, shear-resistant flocs that settle rapidly, mitigating hydraulic surges and guaranteeing compliance with national environmental standards.

Our technical specialists conduct comprehensive on-site jar tests to identify the ideal molecular weight, ionic charge density (anionic, cationic, non-ionic), and optimum dosage rate tailored to your specific effluent matrix.

  • High-rate industrial wastewater clarification
  • Primary, secondary & tertiary sedimentation
  • Superior overflow clarity under variable hydraulic loads
  • On-site jar testing & polymer screening protocols
Solids Handling

Sludge Thickening & Dewatering

Effective sludge conditioning is critical to minimizing hauling logistics and dewatering disposal costs. BESFLOC™ cationic polymers are specifically engineered for mechanical dewatering equipment including high-speed centrifuges, belt filter presses, screw presses, and plate-and-frame filter presses.

Our field engineers optimize polymer maturation times, dilution water hardness, and injection feed points to maximize cake dryness, improve filtrate capture rates, and prevent cloth blinding.

  • High-capture gravity thickening applications
  • Decanter centrifuge & continuous belt press dewatering
  • High-dryness filter press & screw press operations
  • Optimized dosing dilution and aging time controls
Raw Water

Canal & Surface Water Treatment

Raw canal and surface river waters frequently present severe turbidity spikes, organic humic color, and fine colloidal clays. BESFLOC™ clarifying polymers work synergistically with inorganic primary coagulants (alum, PAC, ferric chloride) to sweep out suspended solids rapidly.

Our tailored chemical programs maintain stable plant throughput across monsoon turbidities and winter low-flow conditions, delivering clean clarified feed for downstream RO and softening systems.

  • High-turbidity canal & intake water clarification
  • Colloidal silica, clay & particulate removal
  • Dynamic dosage adaptation for seasonal water swings
  • Full compatibility with polyaluminum chloride (PAC)
Textile Effluent

Dyeing Industry Decolorization

Dyehouse and finishing effluents contain stubborn reactive, disperse, and direct dyes that resist conventional biological treatment. We supply dicyandiamide-based color removal agents and cationic coagulants that destabilize dye molecules, precipitating colorants cleanly without generating unmanageable sludge volumes.

PLUMPK field teams provide on-site technical support across major textile processing clusters in Karachi, Faisalabad, Lahore, and Multan, ensuring zero color bleed in final discharge.

  • Rapid reactive & disperse dye decolorization
  • Chemical oxygen demand (COD) reduction
  • Low-volume, dense precipitate formation
  • Tailored for textile wet-processing mills
