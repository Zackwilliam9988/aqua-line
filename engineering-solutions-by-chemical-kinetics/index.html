Desalination

Reverse Osmosis Systems

PLUMPK designs, supplies, installs, and commissions reverse osmosis plants for industrial, commercial, and municipal applications across Pakistan — from brackish groundwater to seawater desalination.

Our RO projects integrate pre-treatment, membrane systems, and post-treatment with PLC/SCADA automation, performance testing, commissioning, and operator training. PLUMPK also supplies CIP chemicals, antiscalants, and chlorination systems for complete lifecycle support.

  • Brackish water and seawater desalination systems
  • Custom-engineered skid-mounted and containerized plants
  • Pre-treatment, RO, and post-treatment integration
  • PLC/SCADA automation and remote monitoring
  • Performance testing, commissioning, and operator training
  • Ongoing chemical supply and membrane cleaning services
Ion Exchange

Water Softening Plants

Ion exchange-based softening systems protect boilers, heat exchangers, and process equipment from hardness-related scaling and downtime. PLUMPK softeners are designed for reliable hardness removal with minimal operator intervention.

Systems are sized for boiler feed, process water, and utility applications — with regeneration controls, brine systems, and integration into wider plant water balances.

  • Boiler feed and process water softening
  • Automatic regeneration and brine management
  • Skid-mounted packages for fast installation
  • Integration with RO and DM pre-treatment
High Purity

Demineralization Plants

Two-bed and mixed-bed DM plants produce high-purity water for power, pharmaceutical, and electronics industries — engineered to meet specific conductivity and silica targets.

PLUMPK provides complete DM solutions including vessel design, resin selection, regeneration systems, and post-commissioning performance verification.

  • Two-bed and mixed-bed DM configurations
  • Conductivity and silica target engineering
  • Power, pharmaceutical, and electronics applications
  • Commissioning and performance verification
Wastewater

Effluent Treatment Plants

Complete ETP solutions from primary treatment through biological and tertiary polishing — designed to meet NEQS discharge standards for textile, food, chemical, and distillery industries.

PLUMPK links plant engineering with wastewater chemistry supply — including polyelectrolytes and process chemicals — so treatment systems perform reliably from day one.

  • Primary, biological, and tertiary treatment stages
  • NEQS-compliant discharge design
  • Textile, food, chemical, and distillery effluent
  • Integrated chemical supply and jar testing support
