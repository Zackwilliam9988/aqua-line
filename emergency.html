What To Do Before Our Plumber Arrives

Take these 4 quick steps right now to protect your property and family.

1

Shut Off the Main Water Valve

Locate your main stopcock valve (often near the water meter, under the kitchen sink, or outside the main entryway). Turn clockwise (right) until tight to cut off pressurized flow.

2

Relieve Trapped Line Pressure

Open an outside hose spigot or the lowest sink tap in the home. This drains the remaining trapped water safely outside instead of spewing from the burst pipe.

3

Avoid Electrical Contact

If standing water reaches electrical outlets, baseboard wiring, or appliances, DO NOT enter the water. Go to the breaker box and switch off power to affected zones immediately.

4

Send a 5-Second Video on WhatsApp

Take a short video of the leak and send it to our WhatsApp at +923046902756 so our en-route technician prepares exact fittings before arrival.
