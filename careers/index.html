Build Your Career in Water Treatment

At AquaLine Services, our people are our greatest asset. We offer a dynamic environment where engineers, technicians, and sales professionals grow alongside Pakistan's leading water treatment company.

Established in 2002 and owned by specialized engineers, AquaLine is Pakistan's trusted partner for water treatment chemicals, equipment, and turnkey plants. With regional offices in Lahore, Karachi, Rawalpindi, Multan, and Faisalabad, we provide opportunities across technical service, application engineering, sales, and operations — working with world-class partners and more than 400 industrial customers.

Our Culture

Purposeful Work With Room to Grow

AquaLine believes meaningful careers are built on technical excellence, teamwork, and accountability. Our engineers and sales teams work closely with customers on real plant challenges — from jar testing and program optimization to commissioning RO and effluent treatment plants.

We promote from within where possible, invest in skills development, and value a collaborative culture where every team member contributes to our promise: We Promise – We Deliver!
